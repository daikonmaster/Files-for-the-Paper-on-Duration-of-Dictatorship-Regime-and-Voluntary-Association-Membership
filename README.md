Author: Olena Bedasheva
Abstract: This paper seeks to fill the gap between civil society participation and historical legacies in civil society literature. The time spent under the dictatorship regime generates different outcomes in the membership level in voluntary associations. I argue that the longer the consecutive period of the undemocratic governance country has, the lower is the level of association membership in the respective country. The following core databases were used to test this relationship with a global scope: World Values Survey, GWF Autocratic Regimes, and Boix Miller Rosato (BMR). By applying the multilevel empirical strategy, the study reveals the substantial and negative effect of different types of dictatorial governance's duration length on voluntary organisations' involvement.

Keywords: historical institutionalism; voluntary membership; dictatorship; multilevel models.
